## Lines that stayed with me

_From my notes while reading, some paraphrased from memory._

- Because the generations who came before you have been pillaged…
- We tell people to follow their dreams, but you can only dream of…
- Even if he never leaves the ghetto…
- Never let the memory of something painful prevent me from trying.
- As the outsider, you can retreat into a shell.
- I was everywhere with everybody, and at the same time I was all by myself.
- You must be careful with who you surround yourself with.
- Because you are giving the kid tough love when maybe he just needs love.
- If you talk to a man in a language that he understands…
- I understand that you have a culture and an identity that exists beyond me. I see you as a human being.
- Everything I have ever done, I have done from a place of love. If I don't punish you, the world will punish you even worse.
- Because he does not hate us; he hated himself.
- And that's the problem with the world: they themselves cannot police themselves, so they want to police everyone around them.
- You cannot blame your past for who you are. You are responsible for you.
- People say all the time that they would do anything for the people they love. But would you really?
