## Lines I loved from this book

- We tell people to follow their dreams, but you can only dream of what you can imagine, and depending on where you come from, your imagination can be quite limited.

- She would say, "Even if he never leaves the ghetto, he will know that the ghetto is not the world. If that is all I accomplish, I've done enough."

- I never let the memory of something painful prevent me from trying something new. If you think too much about the ass-kicking your mom gave you, you will stop pushing the boundaries and breaking the rules.

- As the outsider, you can retreat into a shell, be anonymous, be invisible. Or you can go the other way. You protect yourself by opening up.

- I was everywhere with everybody, and at the same time I was all by myself.

- You must be careful who you surround yourself with because where you are can determine who you are.

- If you talk to a man in a language that he understands, that goes to his head. If you talk to him in his language, that goes to his heart.

- I understand that you have a culture and identity that exists beyond me. I see you as a human being.

- Everything I have ever done I've done from a place of love. If I don't punish you, the world will punish you even worse. The world doesn't love you.

- "Pray for Abel," she'd say. "Because he doesn't hate us. He hates himself."

- And that's the problem with the world. We have people who cannot police themselves so they want to police everyone else around them.

- You cannot blame your past for who you are. You are responsible for you. You make your own choices.

- People say all the time that they'd do anything for the people they love. But would you really? Would you do anything? Would you give anything? I don't know that a child knows that kind of selfless love. A mother, yes.

- Because the generations before you have been pillaged, rather than being free to use your skills and education to move forward, you lose everything just trying to bring everyone behind you back to zero.
