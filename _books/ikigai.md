## Lines that I reread

- Only staying active will make you want to live a hundred years.

- It is very important to expose yourself to change, even if stepping outside your comfort zone means feeling a bit of anxiety.

- The people who live the longest have two dispositional traits in common: a positive attitude and a high degree of emotional awareness.

- A stoic attitude (serenity in the face of a setback) can also help you keep young, as it lowers anxiety and stress levels and stabilizes behaviour.

- What do we need to be doing right now? What action should we be taking? The key to achieving this is having dared to look inside yourself to find your ikigai.

- You have to accept that the world, like the people who live in it, is imperfect, but that it is still full of opportunities for growth and achievement.

- It is much more important to have a compass pointing to a concrete objective than to have a map.

- Flow is mysterious. It is like a muscle: the more you train it, the more you will grow.

- The secret for a good life is smiling and having a good time.

- A wise person can live with life's pleasures but should always remain conscious of how easy it is to be enslaved by them.
