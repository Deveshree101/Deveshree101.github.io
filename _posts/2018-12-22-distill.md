Here I put down my thoughts on how things work.

Not just how to use them - how they actually work, underneath. That pull shows up everywhere I look: in the systems behind a piece of technology, in what makes an AI model behave the way it does, in the patterns that shape how people and societies move, in the questions philosophy has been circling for centuries. Tech, AI, social science, philosophy - on paper, four different fields. In practice, the same instinct, just pointed in different directions.

Curiosity is the thing that drives me. Not as a personality quirk, but as something closer to a compass. I'm curious about almost everything around me, and I've stopped trying to narrow that down to one "area of interest" - it was never going to be just one thing.

*Notes on Curiosity* is where that curiosity gets somewhere to go. It's not a record of everything I think about that would be impossible to keep up with, and probably exhausting to read. It's a fraction of it: the ideas that stuck, the questions I couldn't let go of, written down instead of left to fade.

If you're the kind of person who also can't resist asking "but why" — welcome. You'll fit right in here.
